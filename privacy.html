Beepika — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Contact: vm2325.kids@gmail.com

Beepika is a game for children aged 2–5. It is published in the Kids category of the App Store.

We collect no data. Beepika has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, tracking, social features or chat. The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information about children or parents, and it sends nothing to us or to third parties. We have no servers.

Stored only on the device. The cars, pets, maps and other progress your child creates are saved only on this device. They are not synced to iCloud and are deleted when you delete the app.

Internet only for purchases. The game works fully offline. The internet is used only by Apple's App Store when an adult buys or restores the one-time purchase “Unlock Everything”. Purchases are available only behind a parental gate. Apple processes the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy; we receive no information about you or your child, only Apple's confirmation that the purchase is active.

No permissions. Beepika does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications.

Crash reports from Apple. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. They do not identify you or your child.

Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR). Because Beepika does not collect personal information from children, no parental consent is required to play. We do not process personal data within the meaning of the GDPR or the UK GDPR. If you write to us by email, we use your address only to reply and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed. You may ask us at any time to delete it.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. The short version of this policy is also available in the app, in the area for parents.
