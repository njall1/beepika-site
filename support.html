Beepika — Support

Contact: vm2325.kids@gmail.com

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game? Write to vm2325.kids@gmail.com — we usually reply within a few days.

Restoring the purchase. Tap the button for parents in the top right corner of the garage, solve the parental gate (type the number written in words) and tap Restore Purchases. “Unlock Everything” is shared with your Family Sharing group.

Ask to Buy. If the purchase waits for approval, the game keeps playing; everything unlocks as soon as the organizer approves it.

No sound? Beepika respects the Ring/Silent switch: turn silent mode off and raise the volume.

Privacy. Beepika collects no data, has no ads or analytics and works offline. Read the privacy policy.
