Beepika: Toddler Car Games

Trucks, Planes, Boats for Kids

Beepika is a gentle first game about cars, planes and boats for children aged 2 to 5. Build your own vehicle, choose where to go and set off. There is no text to read, no timers and no way to lose: every tap answers with a happy beep, a bounce or a sparkle.

BUILD YOUR OWN
• Pick a body, a color, wheels and a decoration — every combination works
• Tune it in the workshop: engine, booster, spoiler, lights and a salute from the exhaust instead of smoke — confetti, fireworks, soap bubbles, hearts, butterflies, music notes and more
• Tap your vehicle and it does a new trick every time — each one has eight of its own; a big button makes it jump for the high stars
• A friendly pet lives in every room: the garage, the hangar and the dock. With a rocket the hangar turns into a space station, with a submarine the dock becomes an underwater base

GO ANYWHERE
• Drive through the forest, along the beach, across snow, desert and fairy lands
• Fly over cities with famous landmarks and fly to every planet of the Solar System — then watch it turn while its moons circle around it
• Sail rivers and seas, dive to coral reefs, sunken ships and crystal caves, and steer the submarine up and down with your finger
• Tap the sun to turn day into night; leaves fall, rainbows shine and shooting stars fly by

TAKE EVERY ANIMAL ALONG
• Animals live by the road, on branches, in the sky and under the water — each kind looks like itself and answers a tap with its own voice
• Pick up any animal and it hops into a trailer, a raft, a basket, an aquarium or a space module; the bus, the plane and the airship carry passengers in their windows
• Kind little aliens float near every planet — the flying saucer lifts them with its beam
• At the finish all your passengers jump out and wave

MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Big buttons, landscape only, no reading needed
• Sounds and gentle vibration answer every touch
• Works offline, anywhere — in the car, on a plane, at grandma's
• VoiceOver labels and Reduce Motion support

FREE TO PLAY, ONE PURCHASE FOR EVERYTHING
Free: the sedan, the small truck, the fire truck and Santa's flying sleigh with two reindeer, all colors, the forest and the beach with their animals, the snowy Christmas Tale map to fly over, night mode, weather and seasons, plus a peek into the dock.
"Unlock Everything" opens all 28 vehicles — cars and special vehicles, planes, rockets and a flying saucer, boats and submarines — every tuning part, wheel and decoration, and all 47 maps: fairy lands, eight cities, the Solar System, seas and the underwater world. One-time purchase, shared with your family through Family Sharing. No subscriptions.

SAFE FOR KIDS
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts
• Progress is saved only on this device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else
• The purchase is behind a parental gate
• No links out of the game
